Problems

1. Technical Skills - Students may lack advanced knowledge in web development, making it difficult to build and maintain the portal without proper training.

2. Limited Resources - Access to reliable internet, hosting services, or quality devices might be restricted, which can slow down development.

3. Time Management - Balancing portal development with academic requirements and other school activities may cause delays.

4. Content Quality - Ensuring that recipes, cooking tips, and other resources are accurate, engaging, and well-presented could be a challenge.

5. Design and Usability - Creating a user-friendly interface that is easy for other students to navigate may require extra effort and testing.

6. Collaboration Issues - If the project involves group work, coordinating schedules and responsibilities among team members might be difficult.

7. Sustainability - Updating and maintaining the portal regularly could be challenging once the initial project is completed.
